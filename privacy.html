🔒 Privacy Policy

At ARVision, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when using our augmented reality services.

Last Updated: August 2025 | Effective Date: Immediately upon acceptance

Data Collection

  • Camera and sensor data for AR functionality
  • Device location when explicitly permitted
  • Usage analytics to improve user experience
  • Account information (email, username)
  • Environmental mapping data (processed locally)

AR-Specific Privacy Protections

  • Real-time image processing occurs on-device
  • No biometric data is stored permanently
  • Environmental scans are anonymized
